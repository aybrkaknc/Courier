Send pages, links, selected text, media and screenshots to Telegram directly through your own bot.

Courier sends what you are looking at to your own Telegram chat, a group or a channel, in one click. Everything goes straight from your browser to Telegram. There is no intermediate server, no account and no telemetry.

WHAT YOU CAN SEND
- The current page (title and link), from the popup, the right-click menu or a keyboard shortcut
- Links, selected text, images, video, audio and linked files, from the right-click menu
- A screenshot of the visible area, or of an area you select

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
- Alt+Shift+1: send the current page
- Alt+Shift+2: screenshot of the visible area
- Alt+Shift+3: screenshot of a selected area
You can change them on chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

SETUP IN A MINUTE
1. Create a bot with @BotFather in Telegram and copy its token.
2. Paste it in Courier's settings. The token is picked out and checked for you.
3. Send your bot a message and press "Find chat ID". Courier fills it in.
4. Save and send a test message.

PRIVACY
- Your bot token and settings stay on your device.
- Content is sent only when you trigger it, only to your own chat, only to the Telegram Bot API.
- The developer receives none of your data.
- Optional: download media in the browser when Telegram cannot fetch it (asks for access to all sites, off by default).

Also: silent sending, link preview removal, English and Turkish interface, light and dark themes.
