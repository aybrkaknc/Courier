Send pages, links, selected text, media and screenshots to Telegram directly through your own bot.

Your content goes straight from your browser to Telegram. There is no intermediate server, no account and no telemetry.

- Send the current page from the popup, the right-click menu or a keyboard shortcut
- Send links, selected text, images, video, audio and linked files from the right-click menu
- Send a screenshot of the visible area or a selected area
- Optional silent sending and link preview removal
- English and Turkish interface, light and dark themes

Setup: create a bot with @BotFather, send it a message, then enter its token and your chat ID in settings.
